Jon's favorite logo — extra

Free-pass front cover A — “The Threshold.” Why: the whole book hangs on this doorway — Harlow on the step at morning, looking back over his shoulder to invite you along, the road already winding away behind him toward the barn, the ballpark lights and the shore.

Free-pass front cover B — “The Road.” Why: the whole journey on one cover — Harlow mid-road looking back for you, and his friends waiting one by one along the way: Chester laughing at the fence, the Hibernator waving at the ballpark gate, Fiona and her three ducklings at the pond, Babs at her tide pool, home glowing small on the horizon.

Free-pass back cover B — “Home at Dusk.” Why: the quiet end of the book — every window lit, two friends watching from inside, and Harlow small on the path, tail up, nearly home. The calm upper sky is left empty on purpose: that's where the blurb lives.
