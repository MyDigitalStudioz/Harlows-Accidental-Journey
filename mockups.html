What do these labels mean?

A quick key to the Mockups page.

  • What a mockup isA quick scene idea to try out the layout: where everyone stands and how the page feels. Faces and fur are rough by design.
  • Who made itThe card color tells you. Lavender = made by RavynMint = made by Trinity
  • FinalsThe finished page is always made with ChatGPT. When a mockup has one, its card shows a “See the final” link.
  • How manyAt most 2–3 mockups per spread in each round, so the page stays easy to look through.
  • ChecksMockups only get layout checks: nothing important in the middle fold, room in the sky for the words, and nothing cut off at the edges.
    Checks passed the layout works.
    Being checked just made, not looked over yet.
  • The dashed boxShows the 2:1 book page. The thin line down the middle is the fold.
  • The dotsA letter dot means that person likes this scene idea. Mockups never go on the showcase; a liked idea gets made as a ChatGPT final first.
  • LettersMockups have their own letters: S07-M1, S07-M2 and so on. They never renumber.
