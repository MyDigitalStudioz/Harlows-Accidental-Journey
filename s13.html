Hi Mom — here's why this page needs new art. In your earlier words, this was Gideon's page: he swooped down, told Harlow he was a city bird who never leaves town, and pointed him toward the ballpark — and the picture we made matches those words exactly. In your new words, Gideon steps aside and a kind bus driver gives Harlow a ride instead. We love the new version — but now the old picture tells a story the words no longer tell. So we spent the evening making new pictures that fit your new words, keeping whatever we could of the old page: the same street, the ballpark at the end of it, Gideon, and Tiger the cat. The current page is first, then every version we made, in the order we made them. Tell us which ones you like!

The new words for this page Feeling let down, I slumped onto a bench near a bus stop. There was an older gentleman sitting there, lost in his newspaper. He peeked over it, smiled, and said, “Do you need any help, little one?” My eyes filled with excitement as I told him I was trying to get to Long Beach. The man exclaimed, “What a coincidence! I’m the bus driver, and Long Beach is my very next stop!” He smirked and said, “Hop on, and enjoy the ride!” “Oh my goodness, thank you!” I replied ecstatically. “I can’t wait to get home!”
